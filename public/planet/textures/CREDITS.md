# Earth texture provenance

These are authentic NASA Earth Observatory source images. No generative imagery is used.
The app derivatives are equirectangular, 2048 × 1024, progressive optimized JPEG at quality 85.
The unmodified downloads are in `original/`; both original and derivative SHA-256 values and URLs are in `manifest.json`.

| Derivative | Observation/product | Attribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| earth-day-2k.jpg | NASA Blue Marble (2002), land/ocean/ice mosaic; mostly June–September 2001 land observations | NASA Goddard Space Flight Center; Reto Stöckli; Robert Simmon; MODIS and supporting data teams |
| earth-clouds-2k.jpg | Historical Blue Marble cloud composite, published 2002; this is a decorative historical cloud mask, not live weather | NASA Goddard Space Flight Center; Reto Stöckli; Robert Simmon; MODIS Atmosphere Group |

- GEBCO grid data are public domain, free of charge, with GEBCO conditions/disclaimer and requested source attribution. This derivative is NASA's GEBCO_08 topographic visualization, not a current GEBCO grid release.
