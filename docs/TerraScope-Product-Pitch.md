# TerraScope — product and launch pitch

## The short pitch

TerraScope is a free window to the world. Take a virtual escape, discover a country's stories and historic places, or get into the football debate with club legends and all-time greats. Start with curiosity; leave with somewhere new to explore.

## The promise

**A small escape. A bigger world.**

Lead with the free escape experience. Country discovery adds depth; football gives people something to share and discuss. The atlas, election watch and trip planner support that experience without competing with the first action.

## Who to reach first

- Students and young people who want an interesting five-minute break.
- Curious travellers researching places before they can visit.
- Football fans sharing and defending their personal top five.
- Teachers and learners looking for a starting point for country and heritage research.

## First launch clips

1. “Your five-minute break, somewhere else.” Open the spinning globe, enter the Jordan Pond panorama and turn the view. End with “Explore for free.”
2. “How much of Nigeria have you actually seen?” Open Lagos, then the country profile and its heritage records. Ask viewers which Nigerian place should get a full 360° tour next.
3. “Pick five. Defend every one.” Build a personal football five, share it, and invite replies. Label it a personal ranking, not a public poll.
4. “You know the name. Do you know the place?” Follow a country into its historic sites and a guided photo journey.

Use @gojo_tk0 for an initial creator-led demonstration if the owner chooses to launch from that account. Do not post or message anyone automatically.

## A small launch sequence

- Invite a small first group to try one escape on a phone and share one discovery or football five.
- Fix the friction they report before widening the launch.
- Publish short screen recordings around one concrete experience per clip.
- Ask for the next destination to prioritise; expand 360° coverage based on actual interest.

## What this release actually includes

- 12 free escapes: four interactive 360° tours and eight guided photo journeys. The Baltimore tour has two connected panorama viewpoints. These are recorded photographs, not live video or navigable simulations of entire countries.
- A rotating Earth, deliberate page reveals, responsive layouts and a global motion pause.
- Country profiles with six additional discovery facts and linked UNESCO heritage records from a checked register of 1,273 active properties.
- Nine exact national polling dates and two expected windows in the reviewed election register. Counters are live; date verification is editorial, not a live feed.
- Editorial men's football lists for 30 selected major clubs, five domestic leagues and 20 global GOAT picks. The opening club selection is not exhaustive.
- Local exploration passport, saved scenes, optional synthetic wind, session timer, share links and personal football fives.

## Boundaries to keep in the pitch

Do not say every country has a 360° tour, that election data updates itself, or that the virtual images are live. The core escapes have no TerraScope payment requirement; visitors still use their normal internet data. On Vercel, saved trips, passport stamps and favourites remain in that browser. The private Sites trip service keeps its existing account-owned records.

## Sustainable growth

Keep the core virtual escape experience free. Potential later revenue, requiring separate product decisions, includes disclosed destination sponsorships, optional paid personal itinerary help, and institution partnerships. Do not introduce a paywall or payment flow as part of this release.

## What to measure after launch

If analytics are introduced later, start with escape opens, tour completion, repeat visits and shares. This release does not invent visitor counts, add analytics tracking or claim traction.
